# Velaris Promotional Video - Gemini Veo Prompt

## Main Prompt (60-second version)

```
Create a sleek, modern tech promotional video for "Velaris" - an AI visibility analysis platform.

STYLE: Clean, minimalist, professional SaaS aesthetic. Dark backgrounds with blue and purple accent colors. Smooth transitions. Modern sans-serif typography.

SEQUENCE:

SCENE 1 (0-8s): THE PROBLEM
- Show a person asking their phone: "What's the best CRM for small business?"
- Phone screen shows ChatGPT responding with competitor names
- Text overlay: "When customers ask AI for recommendations..."
- Mood: Slightly concerning, realization moment

SCENE 2 (8-15s): THE STAKES
- Split screen showing multiple AI interfaces (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity)
- Statistics appearing: "527% growth in AI search" and "70% of searches end without a click"
- Text: "Is AI recommending your competitors instead of you?"
- Mood: Urgency, tension

SCENE 3 (15-30s): THE SOLUTION
- Velaris logo appears with tagline
- Show a clean dashboard interface being used
- User types brand name, competitors appear
- Analysis runs across 3 AI platforms simultaneously
- Results show: visibility score, competitor comparison, journey stages
- Text: "Velaris tests what AI says about your brand"
- Mood: Relief, empowerment

SCENE 4 (30-45s): KEY FEATURES
- Quick cuts showing:
  1. Leaderboard comparing brand vs competitors
  2. Journey stages (Awareness → Consideration → Decision)
  3. AI follow-up questions revealing reasoning
  4. Actionable recommendations
- Text overlays for each feature
- Mood: Capability, sophistication

SCENE 5 (45-55s): SOCIAL PROOF / VALUE
- Show pricing: "Start free, upgrade for $149/mo"
- Comparison: "56% below market average"
- Text: "Know what AI says before your customers ask"
- Mood: Value, accessibility

SCENE 6 (55-60s): CTA
- Velaris logo centered
- URL: velaris.ai
- Text: "Check your AI visibility - Free"
- Button animation
- Mood: Action, invitation

MUSIC: Upbeat electronic, building momentum, modern tech feel
COLORS: Primary blue (#3B82F6), accent purple (#8B5CF6), dark slate (#1E293B), white
FONT: Clean sans-serif (Inter or similar)
```
